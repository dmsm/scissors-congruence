Scissors Congruence

An interactive demonstration of the Wallace–Bolyai–Gerwien theorem.

by Dima Smirnov and Zivvy Epstein

Click or tap to begin drawing the first polygon.

Theorem

Any simple polygon can be cut into a finite number of pieces and rearranged into any other simple polygon of equal area.
